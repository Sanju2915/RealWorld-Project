# Real-world Data Project: Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Objective
This project uses a custom retail sales dataset to perform a simple end-to-end business analysis. The goal is to understand sales performance, profit contribution, customer behavior, and regional trends using a realistic business scenario.

## Selected Domain
Retail

## Dataset Overview
The dataset is stored in `data/retail_sales_dataset.csv` and contains 36 sales records from April to May 2026.

## Dataset Attributes
- `order_id`: unique order identifier
- `order_date`: date of purchase
- `region`: business region
- `city`: city where the order was placed
- `category`: product category
- `product_name`: item sold
- `sales_channel`: online or store purchase
- `customer_segment`: Regular, Premium, or New customer
- `unit_price`: price per unit before discount
- `quantity`: number of units sold
- `discount_pct`: discount percentage applied
- `payment_method`: Card, UPI, Cash, or EMI
- `sales_amount`: final billed amount
- `cost_amount`: estimated business cost
- `profit`: profit earned on the order

## Key Findings
- Total sales reached `Rs. 437,173.50`.
- Total profit reached `Rs. 127,473.50`.
- Overall profit margin was `29.16%`.
- Average order value was `Rs. 12,143.71`.
- Electronics was the best-performing category with `Rs. 287,796.00` in sales.
- The North region generated the highest revenue with `Rs. 156,577.00`.
- Store sales slightly outperformed online sales.
- Premium customers contributed the highest segment revenue.

## Conclusion
The retail business performs strongest in high-value electronics products and in the North region. Premium customers generate the largest revenue share, showing the importance of customer retention and targeted marketing. The dataset suggests that a stronger focus on profitable electronics inventory, premium customer loyalty, and region-specific campaigns could improve future business performance.
